# Artwork prompts

The artwork is decorative. The recipe places all chart and presenter content.
Do not put charts, names, logos, numbers, or interface labels in the artwork.

The following original prompts produced the bundled artwork on 2026-10-08.
They describe an original interpretation of the palette, not a copy of another brand's assets.

## Background: assets/background.png

```text
Create one original production background asset for a premium trading livestream called Funded Desk. Landscape 16:9 composition, ideally 1536x864. This is a BACKGROUND TEXTURE, not a screenshot or mockup, and must contain no text, letters, numbers, logos, people, screens, charts, cards or chips. Visual direction: warm ivory #F5F1EB, near-black #111111 used extremely sparingly, brushed champagne gold #BD852E/#F0C970/#C48E36 accents. Calm editorial luxury, architectural photography mood. Very faint soft-focus city-window silhouettes and subtle paper/plaster texture, low contrast. Keep the central 90 percent of the image almost uniformly warm ivory and visually quiet so real OBS chart and presenter sources can sit on it. Concentrate delicate gold architectural glints only along outermost corners and lower edge, never large decorative shapes. Soft natural daylight, restrained, elegant, flat full-bleed background. Original artwork inspired by a cream/black/gold editorial palette, no existing brand marks, no watermark. Opaque background.
```

## Dark background: assets/background-dark.png

```text
Create one original opaque background texture for the DARK MODE of a premium trading livestream called Funded Desk. Landscape 16:9, ideally1536x864. Flat full-bleed production background, NOT a screenshot or mockup. Deep warm charcoal #101110 and near-black #080A09, restrained brushed champagne-gold accents #BD852E and #C48E36. Elegant editorial studio architecture, subtle matte plaster and faint smoked-glass window silhouettes at the extreme edges. Central90percent almost uniformly very dark and low contrast to hold real OBS chart and presenter sources. Sparse thin gold architectural glints along outermost corners and lower edge only. Quiet premium lighting, clean calm dark stage, no neon, no blue/cyan, no brown grunge, no glowing neon panels. NO text, letters, numbers, logos, people, screens, charts, cards, chips or watermarks. Original artwork based on a cream/black/gold editorial palette translated to dark mode. Opaque background.
```

Only the selected background is copied. The frame is shared by both themes.

## Frame: assets/frame.png

```text
Create an original transparent PNG overlay for a premium trading livestream. Landscape 16:9, ideally 1536x864. This is a production asset: the entire central area MUST be fully transparent alpha, not white, not black, no checkerboard baked in. Design a very restrained luxury frame with hairline brushed champagne-gold (#BD852E, #F0C970, #C48E36) and near-black #111111 accents ONLY at the extreme outer corners and outermost edges, no decoration more than 18 pixels inward at 1536 pixels wide. Fine geometric corner brackets, subtle metallic texture, elegant editorial style matching warm ivory backgrounds. No panels, no inner windows, no words, no letters, no logos, no numbers, no charts, no shadows or haze across center. Minimal original artwork, flat front view, sharp clean edges, no existing brand marks. Preserve true alpha transparency everywhere apart from the thin edge ornament.
```

Asset generation is optional and manual. This command never calls an image service.
Keep replacements as PNG files, then preview the full composition.
