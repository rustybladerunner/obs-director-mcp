## Original arrangements for a trading broadcast

The first arrangement adapts the inspected tournament frames. The other two are original extensions of the production format.
Poker graphics do not establish trading performance or probabilities.
Use supplied values and declare their units, source time, and paper/live status.

1. **Featured decision.** Give the chart the main action area. Put the presenter and guest/replay in the right column.
   Use compact action cards for the instrument, current state, and the latest observed event.
   Keep the session summary below the chart. This fits the existing Funded Desk geometry.
   An alternative uses mirrored cards beneath the chart, with shared session totals in a separate lower strip, following the WSOP grouping.
2. **Session board.** During a break, enlarge the session summary and reduce the chart to a reference panel.
   Separate realized P&L, unrealized P&L, and declared risk units. Show missing values as unavailable.
   Order rows by an explicit rule, such as instrument name or event time. Do not invent competitive rankings.
3. **Replay review.** Give a recorded chart segment the central area and keep the presenter visible beside it.
   Show a persistent REPLAY label and the recording time. Place a concise event card beneath the replay.
   Keep temporary audience alerts in a separate reserved area, clear of chart prices and the replay label.

Use the existing cream/black/gold palette or its dark variant for all three arrangements.
Build new panels from original shapes and typography. Keep the broadcaster names in reference documentation only.
